Elliot Parker

Elliot James Parker

Elliot is the kind of kid who seems incapable of taking anything seriously. He's loud. He's impulsive. He's constantly making jokes. He talks when he should be quiet. He asks questions at the worst possible moments. He wears his baseball cap backwards almost constantly, even though there's absolutely no practical reason for it. He's the kid who would look at an abandoned building and say: "Well, statistically, this is probably where we die." But underneath all of that is a kid who is terrified. He's terrified of infected. He's terrified of violent people. He's terrified of losing people. But most of all, he's terrified of being alone. His humor is essentially armor. If he's talking, he's not thinking about how scared he is. If he's making someone laugh, they're still there. If someone's annoyed with him, at least they're paying attention to him.

  • Nickname: Eli or Elliot
  • Age: 13 at the beginning
  • Birthplace: Fort Meridian
  • Hobbies: Music, Collecting useless things and Telling stories
  • Role: Survivor / scout / later community member
