Nico Volkov

Nikolai Aleksandr Volkov

Nico is almost the exact opposite of Elliot. Quiet. Serious. Suspicious. Observant. Extremely difficult to read. He doesn't waste words. He doesn't trust easily. He doesn't forgive easily. And when he decides someone he loves is in danger, his moral boundaries become frighteningly flexible. Nico learned survival far too young. When he was ten, the survivor community he lived with was attacked by a group of violent people. Everyone he knew was killed or scattered. His little sister, Alina, was taken away. Nico survived. And for three years afterward, he searched for her. That search became the center of his existence.
