Nikolai "Nico" Volkov

At 13, he's just Nico—a quiet kid with a buzz cut who looks older than he is. Years later, hearing someone say “Volkov” could immediately carry weight. He's not naturally cruel. He's defensive. His aggression comes from years of learning that hesitation has consequences. He's observant, suspicious, and uncomfortable with affection. He rarely explains himself. His biggest flaw is that he thinks being useful is the same thing as being worthy of love. And his biggest contradiction is that, despite everything, he keeps protecting people.
