Survivors

Setting: 2035, twenty years after a fungal infection devastated the world in 2015. The infection spread rapidly through human populations, collapsing governments and transforming infected people into increasingly dangerous creatures. Over the following decades, humanity fractured into military-controlled settlements, independent communities, traveling groups, extremist factions, and people who simply survive from day to day. The story begins inside Fort Meridian, a heavily militarized settlement where Elliot Foster, 13, has spent his entire life. Elliot has never experienced the apocalypse himself. To him, the walls, soldiers, rules, ration cards and patrols are simply normal. He's the son of one of the settlement's highest-ranking military officers, so he's grown up with considerably more comfort than most people. He's also constantly breaking the rules. One day, Elliot sneaks beyond the supervised perimeter. At the same time, a rebel assault begins against Fort Meridian. The fighting draws infected toward the settlement, and Elliot becomes stranded outside the protected area. He should have died. Instead, he is rescued by Nikolai "Nico" Volkov, another thirteen-year-old who has spent three years surviving alone after his community was massacred by humans. The two boys hide together until the attack ends. When they return to Fort Meridian, they discover that the settlement has been abandoned or destroyed. Elliot's parents are nowhere to be found. Nico intends to leave. Elliot follows him. Nico repeatedly tells him to go away. Elliot refuses. Eventually, Nico gives up. And that is where their story truly begins. Over the following years, they travel through the remains of the old world, encountering infected, violent survivors, military factions, rebels, settlements, cults and people who demonstrate that humanity can be just as terrifying as the creatures outside the walls. Nico is searching for one thing: his little sister, Alina. Elliot, meanwhile, slowly discovers that the boy he followed because he was afraid of being alone has become the closest thing he's ever had to a family. The story follows them from childhood into adulthood as they lose people, gain friends, make terrible choices, survive impossible situations and slowly become each other's home.
